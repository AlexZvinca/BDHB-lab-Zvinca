I used the spring layout from NetworkX with a random seed of 42. Genes are colored according to their Louvain modules from Week 6, and the most connected genes are displayed as larger nodes.

The network contains 34 genes, 51 connections, and 9 modules. The main hub genes are AIF1 and DNASE1L3, each with 7 connections.

Visualization makes it easier to identify gene modules, highly connected genes, and relationships that are difficult to see in a correlation matrix. Unlike numerical analysis alone, a network graph shows the overall structure clearly. However, gene co-expression does not necessarily mean direct biological interaction.
