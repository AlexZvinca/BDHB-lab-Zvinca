Which method was most appropriate?

I think that K-means is the best method for this dataset because we want to compare two main groups (benign and malignant). It is simple to use and its results can be visualized with PCA. Hierarchical clustering also helps show similarities between samples, while DBSCAN can identify unusual samples but may classify many as noise.

How does clustering compare to phylogenetic trees?

Both clustering and phylogenetic trees group biological data based on similarities. Clustering finds groups of similar samples, while phylogenetic trees try to explain evolutionary relationships. A clustering dendrogram does not necessarily represent evolution.
